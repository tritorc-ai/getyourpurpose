history Our Origins

From Precision Tools to Guiding Futures

Our journey began in 1989 in Mumbai as 'Tristar Equipment', with impact sockets as our very first product. For decades, we engineered precision instruments with an unyielding commitment to quality and structural integrity.

Yet, as our global footprint expanded, our founder realized that the most important structures we build aren't made of steel, but of human potential. Driven by a deep-seated mission to give back, we pivoted our legacy of precision towards guiding students—providing the essential tools needed to craft a meaningful, purposeful life.

water_damage

A Story of Resilience

In August 2017, during the devastating Mumbai monsoon floods, our commitment was put to the ultimate test. While the city stood still, our team waded through waist-deep water to ensure critical equipment reached IOCL Mathura within 48 hours.

This is the "backbone of Tritorc" — a culture of unwavering reliability and human spirit that we now bring to every student we mentor.
