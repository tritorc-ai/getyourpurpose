Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Counselling, admission guidance and the grant are funded by Tritorc. A student never pays us anything.

Who is eligible?

Students based in India who have completed Class 12 or equivalent and are heading into engineering.

What documents do I need?

Marksheets and an income certificate at the verification stage. Nothing is needed to start the application.

How much support is given?

It is decided per student. Our first supported student received ₹14 lakh toward an engineering degree at Thadomal Shahani Engineering College, Mumbai.

Is the grant renewable?

Some grants are one-time and some continue year to year, contingent on maintaining a CGPA of 7. Tritorc decides per student.

Can my parents contact you?

Please do. Most of our conversations about fees happen with the family present.
